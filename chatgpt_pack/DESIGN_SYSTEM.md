# DESIGN_SYSTEM.md

PhotoSense AI should feel like a cinematic, editorial, premium photography product. The interface is a darkroom-inspired critique workspace, not a generic AI chatbot or SaaS dashboard.

## Visual Direction

- Cinematic and photography-focused
- Editorial, structured, and calm
- Premium but restrained
- Darkroom-inspired surfaces with warm off-white text
- Thin borders, subtle grid lines, and carefully controlled yellow accents
- Photos should be treated as the visual center whenever possible

## Typography

- Use elegant Chinese typography.
- Major headings should feel editorial and magazine-like.
- Body and UI labels should remain clear, modern, and readable.
- Small metadata labels may use uppercase styling, letter spacing, or compact label treatment.

## Spacing And Layout

- Use generous spacing and clear hierarchy.
- Avoid overcrowded controls and dense dashboard-style panels.
- Let each page have a clear product purpose and readable structure.
- Image-led sections should feel curated, not decorative.

## What To Avoid

- Generic SaaS dashboard feeling
- Crypto dashboard aesthetics
- Colorful gradient cards
- Childish or overly playful icons
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Random decorative geometry without product meaning
- Report layouts that feel like plain text dumps

## Report Design Principles

- Critique report sections should feel professional and structured.
- The uploaded photo should remain central to the diagnosis experience.
- Scores, annotations, edit guidance, and suggestions should support photographic learning.
- Text should be concise, educational, specific, and constructive.
- “本次评价基准” should read as a compact editorial context card, not a colorful dashboard widget.
- The verdict panel is the user’s primary result area. Its language should be concise, editorial, and photo-specific, not procedural or system-like.
